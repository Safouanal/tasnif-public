Start with the media that matters

When iPhone storage feels tight, the Photos library can be hard to understand. Tasnif focuses on the signal that matters most: occupied space. It highlights large videos and other media that can make a real difference, then lets you review before deleting.

Tasnif is not an automatic delete button. It is a local assistant for understanding your library, checking the known size of a selection and keeping control over the final Photos confirmation flow.

What Tasnif helps you review

  • Large videos and screen recordings.
  • Screenshots, RAW files and old large videos.
  • Probable duplicates that should be checked before action.
