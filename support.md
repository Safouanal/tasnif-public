# Tasnif Support

Last updated: 2026-05-26

Tasnif helps you see what takes space in your Photos library, starting with large videos and other media that can quickly fill an iPhone.

## App Store Status

Tasnif 1.1 has been submitted to Apple for App Review.

The submitted 1.1 release is free and does not include ads, ad SDKs, in-app purchases, accounts, analytics SDKs, tracking, or photo uploads.

## Get Help

If you need help, contact:

support@tasnif.app

Please include:

- your iPhone or iPad model;
- your iOS version;
- the Tasnif version;
- what you were trying to do;
- a screenshot if it helps explain the issue.

Do not send private photos or videos unless you choose to. Tasnif support does not need your media to understand most issues.

For general contact, use:

contact@tasnif.app

## Common Questions

### Does Tasnif upload my photos or videos?

No. Tasnif checks the Photos library you authorize directly on your device. The app does not collect, upload or sell your photos, videos or library metadata.

### Why does a size sometimes stay in progress?

Some iCloud media may not be fully available on the device yet. Tasnif shows the library immediately, then updates sizes progressively when Photos provides the needed information.

### Can Tasnif delete media without asking?

No. Deletions go through Apple's Photos confirmation. Deleted items then follow the standard Photos behavior, including Recently Deleted.

### What if I allowed only some photos?

Tasnif respects limited Photos access. It only shows and checks the photos and videos you authorized. You can change access in iOS Settings at any time.

## Questions frequentes

### Est-ce que Tasnif envoie mes photos ou videos ?

Non. Tasnif verifie la phototheque que vous autorisez directement sur votre appareil. L'app ne collecte pas, n'envoie pas et ne vend pas vos photos, videos ou metadonnees de phototheque.

### Pourquoi une taille peut rester en verification ?

Certains medias iCloud ne sont pas encore totalement disponibles sur l'appareil. Tasnif affiche la phototheque tout de suite, puis met les tailles a jour progressivement quand Photos fournit les informations necessaires.

### Est-ce que Tasnif peut supprimer sans demander ?

Non. Les suppressions passent par la confirmation de Photos. Les elements supprimes suivent ensuite le comportement normal de Photos, dont Supprimes recemment.

### Et si j'ai autorise seulement certaines photos ?

Tasnif respecte l'acces Photos limite. L'app affiche et verifie uniquement les photos et videos que vous avez autorisees. Vous pouvez modifier cet acces dans les reglages iOS a tout moment.
