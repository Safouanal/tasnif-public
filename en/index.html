Common questions

Tasnif answers searches like iPhone storage full, see iPhone photo size, find large videos and clean up Photos safely.

How do I free up iPhone photo storage?

Start with the media that takes the most space: long videos, screen recordings, RAW files, screenshots and probable duplicates.

Why did storage not come back after deleting photos?

Deleted media can remain in Recently Deleted. Check what you removed before permanent deletion.

Does Tasnif replace Photos?

No. Tasnif makes occupied space easier to understand, then leaves deletion to the standard Apple Photos flow.
