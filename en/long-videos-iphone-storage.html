Long videos

One long video can take more space than many photos

A concert, game, recorded meeting or vacation clip can stay in Photos and take a large share of storage. Tasnif helps start with those visible files.

Download freeSee what to check

Why long videos come first

Duration changes everything. Even a normal-looking video can become large when it lasts several minutes. Reviewing a few long videos is often more useful than deleting many small photos at random.

Tasnif helps you see those media before acting. Keep important memories, spot temporary videos, then decide only when the choice is clear.

What to review first

  • Long clips from events, trips, classes, meetings or demos.
  • Videos recorded to share information once.
  • Older long videos already saved elsewhere or easy to replace.

Deletion remains voluntary. Tasnif prepares the review, then Apple Photos asks for confirmation.
