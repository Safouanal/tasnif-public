Live Photos

Live Photos can take more space than still photos

A Live Photo keeps a still image and a short motion moment. When many pile up, they can explain part of Photos storage without looking obvious in the grid.

Download freeSee what to check

Why Live Photos deserve a review

Live Photos are not a problem by themselves. They simply keep more than a still image. In a large library, batches of Live Photos taken by habit can become visible storage.

Tasnif helps surface them with the other media worth reviewing. The goal is to see what really takes space, then keep the memories that matter without deleting at random.

What to check first

  • Live Photo series taken during an event or trip.
  • Live Photos that are very close to a still photo you already keep.
  • Older batches you never rewatch but still keep in Photos.

Your media stays on device. Tasnif prepares the review, and Photos asks for confirmation if you choose to delete.
