What a photo cleanup app should respect

Before giving Photos access, it is reasonable to ask where images go, whether they are analyzed online and whether deletion can happen too quickly. Tasnif answers that trust moment with a local and progressive approach.

Photos and videos stay on device. Tasnif helps surface large media, screenshots, RAW files and probable duplicates, then leaves deletion to the standard Apple Photos confirmation.

Signals to look for

  • No account required to understand the library.
  • No upload of photos, videos, thumbnails or Photos library metadata to a Tasnif server.
  • No silent deletion: Photos asks for confirmation.
  • A simple value: see what actually takes space before acting.
