Why these files pile up

Screen recordings are easy to forget: quick tutorials, bug captures, recorded calls, demos sent to someone else. They stay in Photos and can be heavy, especially when they are long or high resolution.

Tasnif helps start with this high-impact media. It keeps media on device, shows known occupied space and lets you review before deciding.

What to check first

  • Long or old screen recordings.
  • 4K videos, slow-motion clips and videos shared once.
  • Video captures kept after a demo or message.
