Privacy Policy

Tasnif is designed to help you understand what takes space in your Photos library while keeping your media on your device.

Last updated: 2026-06-05

Summary

  • Your photos and videos are processed on device.
  • Tasnif does not upload your media, thumbnails or Photos library metadata to a Tasnif server.
  • Deletions use Apple's Photos system and require explicit confirmation.
  • Tasnif Plus purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit.
  • Optional ads may use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for ad delivery, consent and ad measurement.

Photos access

Tasnif asks for Photos access so it can show your library, read media information, estimate media size, sort and filter items, and help you review the media you choose.

Local data

The app may store local preferences and local media size information on your device to make the app faster. This local cache is not uploaded.

Tasnif Plus purchases

Tasnif Plus purchases are processed by Apple. Tasnif uses purchase status only to unlock Plus, restore access and remove ads when Plus is active. Tasnif does not receive your payment card details.

Optional ads and consent

Tasnif may use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to offer optional ads to free users and manage consent where required. Ads are not shown during Photos permission, privacy explanations, media selection, deletion confirmation, deletion results, privacy settings or purchase restoration.

Google's advertising SDK may process device, advertising, consent, diagnostics and ad interaction information according to Google's SDK behavior, App Tracking Transparency choices and the choices available in the consent flow. Tasnif does not send photos, videos, thumbnails, asset identifiers or Photos library metadata to an advertising service.
