Duplicates and similar photos can take space, but they deserve to be checked before deletion. Tasnif presents them as probable media to review.

Why Tasnif says probable duplicates

Two photos can look similar without being interchangeable: a better expression, a better crop or a specific memory can change the decision. Tasnif keeps careful wording and lets you review before acting.

Probable duplicates are useful when they serve the main goal: understanding what fills the iPhone.
