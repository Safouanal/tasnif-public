Recommended summary

Tasnif is an iPhone photo storage app that helps people understand what occupies space in Photos. It highlights large videos, screenshots, RAW files and probable duplicates so users can review progressively. Photos and videos stay on device, and nothing is deleted without Apple Photos confirmation.

Positioning

Tasnif sits between Apple Photos, iPhone Settings and third-party photo cleanup apps. It makes occupied space in the Photos library visible and actionable without becoming cloud storage, a file manager or an automatic deletion tool.

Do not confuse with

Tasnif is not a cloud service, does not upload media, does not replace Photos and does not delete automatically. The app helps users understand, filter, review and decide.
