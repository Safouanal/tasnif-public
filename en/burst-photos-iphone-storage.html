Burst photos

Burst photos can leave many similar images behind

A burst taken to catch the right moment can stay in Photos with several almost identical images. When bursts pile up, they deserve a calm review.

Download freeSee what to check

Why bursts are easy to forget

Burst photos are useful in the moment: moving kids, sports, pets, group shots or fast gestures. The problem comes later, when the whole series stays in the library even though one or two images may be enough.

Tasnif helps find those batches and similar photos without uploading your media. The goal is not to delete quickly, but to make the decision visible.

What to check first

  • Bursts taken only to choose one good photo.
  • Series that duplicate a still photo or Live Photo you already keep.
  • Older batches from camera tests or past events.

Nothing is deleted automatically. Keep the important images, then Photos asks for confirmation if you delete the rest.
