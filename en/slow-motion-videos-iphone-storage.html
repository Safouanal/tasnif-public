Slow-motion videos

Slow-motion videos can quietly fill Photos

A slow-motion clip kept after an event or camera test can stay in the library for a long time. Reviewing it with large videos is often more useful than deleting small photos.

Download freeSee what to check

Why slow-motion clips deserve their own review

Slow-motion clips are easy to forget because they look like regular videos in the Photos grid. Some can still take a lot of space, especially when they are long or recorded at high quality.

Tasnif starts with high-impact media so you do not have to delete at random. Review the heavy videos first, then decide what still matters.

What to check first

  • Very long slow-motion clips or clips kept after one use.
  • Sports, event, pet or camera-test clips you never rewatch.
  • Slow-motion clips that duplicate a normal video from the same moment.

Nothing is deleted automatically. Tasnif helps you find media, and Photos asks for confirmation if you choose to delete.
