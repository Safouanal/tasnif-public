Privacy and deletion

Does Tasnif upload my photos or videos?

No. Tasnif reviews the Photos library you authorize directly on your iPhone or iPad. Photos, videos, thumbnails and Photos library metadata are not uploaded to a Tasnif server.

Can Tasnif delete media without asking?

No. Tasnif prepares the review, but deletion goes through Photos and requires the standard Apple confirmation.

Storage

Why does Tasnif start with large videos?

Long videos, 4K clips, slow motion and screen recordings can take much more space than many small photos. Reviewing them first helps you quickly understand what fills the iPhone.

Does Tasnif replace iCloud?

No. Tasnif is not a cloud storage service. It helps you see what really occupies the Photos library before deciding what to keep, delete or store elsewhere.

Free, ads and Plus

What is Tasnif Plus for?

Tasnif Plus removes ads and unlocks a more comfortable review experience with more filters, local progress and complete batches.

Are ads required?

No. Ads are an optional exchange for continuing free review on small additional batches. Tasnif Plus removes ads.
