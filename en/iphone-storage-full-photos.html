What to do when Photos fills your iPhone storage

Before buying more storage or deleting random photos, start by seeing which videos, screenshots and media actually take the most room.

Download freeSee priorities

Do not start by deleting at random

When iOS says storage is low, deleting a few recent photos can feel productive but may recover very little. The largest wins are often long videos, screen recordings, slow-motion clips, RAW files and groups of screenshots.

Tasnif helps surface those priorities while keeping media on your device. You can understand occupied space, review a selection, then let Photos ask for final confirmation if you choose to delete.

Suggested review order

  • Start with large videos and screen recordings.
  • Check RAW files, slow-motion videos and older large media.
  • Then review screenshots, probable duplicates and lower-priority photos.

This keeps cleanup calm and concrete. The important signal is not only how many photos you have, but how much space the largest media occupy.
