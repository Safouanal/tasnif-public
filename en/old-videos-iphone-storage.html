Old videos

Old videos can still take a lot of space

Clips saved for years often stay in Photos without being rewatched. When iPhone storage is tight, starting with those videos makes cleanup easier.

Download freeSee what to check

Why start with old videos

A photo library often keeps videos from old events, trips, camera tests or finished shares. They can be long, high resolution and hard to notice among thousands of photos.

Tasnif turns that review into a clear decision: find the videos that may matter for storage, review them with context, then choose what still deserves to stay.

What to check first

  • Old long videos you probably will not rewatch.
  • Camera tests, failed takes or temporary clips kept by habit.
  • Videos already saved elsewhere or replaced by a better version.

Your media stays on device. Nothing is deleted automatically, and Apple Photos asks for confirmation if you decide to make space.
