See the size of videos on iPhone

A single 4K video or long screen recording can take more space than many photos. Tasnif makes those sizes easier to see so you know where to start.

Why video size matters

Videos are often the fastest way to recover storage on an iPhone. Slow-motion clips, 4K videos, long vacation clips and screen recordings can take a lot of room without being obvious in Photos.

Tasnif naturally starts with high-impact media. You can find large videos, see the known space in a view and review items before deciding.
