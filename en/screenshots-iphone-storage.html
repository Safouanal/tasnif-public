Why screenshots are worth checking

A screenshot often has a short useful life. It helps save information, send proof or keep an idea, then it stays in Photos. As the library grows, these items become hard to review from Photos alone.

Tasnif helps isolate this kind of media in a local review. Photos and videos stay on the iPhone or iPad, and deletion still requires the standard Apple Photos confirmation.

What to review first

  • Old screenshots that are no longer useful.
  • Heavy screenshots, long pages and imported images.
  • Captures already saved elsewhere or shared once.

The point is not to delete every screenshot. A careful cleanup starts by seeing which captures really explain the occupied space.
