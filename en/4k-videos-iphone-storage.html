4K videos

4K videos can fill Photos very quickly

When an iPhone is low on space, a few 4K videos can matter more than hundreds of small photos. It helps to find them before deleting at random.

Download freeSee what to check

Why start with 4K videos

4K videos, slow-motion clips and long recordings keep a lot of information. Even if you do not have many of them, they can explain a large part of Photos storage.

Tasnif turns that into a simple review: see large media, check what still matters, then decide calmly.

Videos worth reviewing first

  • Long 4K videos you probably will not rewatch.
  • Slow-motion clips, event videos and high-resolution vacation clips.
  • Screen recordings kept after a share or demo.

Deletion remains voluntary and goes through Apple Photos confirmation. Tasnif helps you see before acting.
