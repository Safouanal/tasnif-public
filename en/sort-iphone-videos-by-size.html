Sort by size

Sorting iPhone videos by size helps you know where to start

When Photos takes too much space, deleting random files rarely gives the best result. It helps to see the videos that are actually heavy.

Download freeSee the method

Why sort by size

A long or 4K video can take more space than many photos. Starting with the heaviest files saves time and avoids deleting memories at random.

Tasnif keeps the review local on device. See what to check, keep what matters, then confirm in Photos only if you want to delete.

Videos to check first

  • Long or old videos you have not rewatched.
  • Screen recordings and temporary demos.
  • 4K videos, slow-motion clips and large vacation videos.

Sorting is not about deleting everything. It is a calmer way to see where the real storage gain is.
