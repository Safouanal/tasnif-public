Clean up your iPhone photo library without automatic deletion

Download TasnifSee the method

Cleanup should start with understanding

Tasnif shows useful categories: large videos, screenshots, screen recordings, RAW files, old videos and probable duplicates. Each category answers a simple question: does this media deserve to take this much space?

The app does not promise magic deletion. It prepares the review, shows known space and leaves the final confirmation to Photos.
